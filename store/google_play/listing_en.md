## Full description (Play limit: 4000 characters)

Rafeeq Al-Darb is a complete Islamic companion for your day: the Qur'an, prayer times and the adhan, adhkar, hadith collections, tajweed lessons and the rites of Hajj and Umrah — all from documented sources credited inside the app, with no ads and no tracking.

📖 The Holy Qur'an
• Six printings of the mushaf, as page images and as text, working offline once downloaded.
• Pages turn right to left like a printed mushaf, whatever the app's language.
• Translations of the meanings in 45 languages.
• Complete recitations to listen to or download, and the recitation of any ayah you select.
• A khatma tracker that follows your progress day by day.

🕌 Prayer and adhan
• Prayer times for your location, with a choice of calculation method and manual corrections.
• The adhan on time in the voices of several muezzins, on a calm adhan screen that shows the words in step with the voice for the synced recordings.
• Qibla compass, and reminders before and after the prayer and at the iqama.
• Hijri date, and "on this day" in both the Hijri and Gregorian calendars.

📿 Adhkar and tasbih
• Morning and evening, sleep and waking, after prayer, travel and more — from Imam al-Nawawi's "al-Adhkar", with their sources.
• A tasbih counter for 33, 100, 1000, no limit or a number you choose, with a vibration at each round.

📚 Hadith and library
• The nine hadith books built into the app: 67,153 hadiths, arranged by chapter and searchable, with nothing to download.
• The Encyclopedia of Translated Prophetic Hadiths, with explanation and word meanings, in seven languages.
• A library of 213 books in tafsir, hadith, fiqh, seerah and spirituality; 83 of them can be read aloud.
• Search by title, by author, and inside the books' text.

🎓 Learning
• Tajweed in three levels — Tuhfat al-Atfal, al-Muqaddimah al-Jazariyyah and al-Tamhid — with the points of articulation, and every rule heard in an ayah of the Qur'an.
• Hajj and Umrah step by step from Imam al-Nawawi's "al-Idah", with interactive drawings of tawaf, sa'i and the jamarat.
• A guide for new Muslims.

✨ And more
• Ruqyah recitations to listen to.
• Dedications: dedicate a reading, istighfar or tasbih to someone you love.
• Focus mode, and reminders for the sunnah surahs and the recommended fasts.
• Sync your khatma, progress and counters across your devices.
• Light and dark themes, and fifteen Arabic fonts including traditional Islamic faces.
• Interface in seven languages: Arabic, English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian and Urdu.

🔒 No ads, no tracking
Ads were refused on purpose, so that nothing unfitting ever appears beside an ayah, and no data about you is collected. The app is entirely free, and nothing in it is behind a payment.
